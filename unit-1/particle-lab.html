The Flat Parts

Predict first

While the ice is melting, you keep the flame on. What does the thermometer do?

Why the line refuses to climb

Temperature measures how fast the particles are moving, on average.

During a flat section, the energy going in is not making particles move faster. It is being used to break the attractions holding the particles together. Pulling particles apart takes energy, and while that job is being done, the speed does not change, so the thermometer does not change either.

Once every particle has been freed, the energy goes back to speeding them up, and the line climbs again.

On the graphWhat the energy is doing
Going upMaking the particles move faster, so the temperature rises
FlatPulling the particles apart from each other, so the temperature stays the same
A cooling curve is the same graph in reverse

Remove energy instead of adding it, and the line goes down, then flat while the substance freezes or condenses, then down again. The flat parts are still phase changes. The attractions are re-forming instead of breaking.
