Stuck on boiling versus evaporation?

Both are a liquid turning into a gas. Boiling happens throughout the whole liquid once it reaches its boiling point, which is why you see bubbles. Evaporation happens slowly, only at the surface, and at temperatures well below boiling. A puddle drying up is evaporation. A kettle is boiling.
