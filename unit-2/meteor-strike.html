Real world

Chelyabinsk, Russia, 2013. A space rock about 20 m wide hit the air at about 19 km/s. It exploded high in the sky, and the blast broke windows across the city.

NASA DART, 2022. NASA crashed a spacecraft into a small asteroid on purpose, at about 6 km/s. The hit changed the asteroid’s path. It was a test to protect Earth one day.
